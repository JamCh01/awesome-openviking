# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Awesome OpenViking to be a welcoming, useful place for builders of every background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good faith, and focus feedback on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints, experience levels, and identities.
- Credit other people's work and disclose your relationship to a submission.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and inclusion.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub message to the repository owner rather than posting private details in an issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, suspend participation, or report behavior to GitHub, proportionately to the situation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and when representing the project in other spaces.
